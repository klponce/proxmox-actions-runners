Create your GitHub App

The proxmox-actions-runners installer sent you here. It needs a GitHub App to register your self-hosted runners. The App is yours: private, owned by the account you choose, with one permission, and no webhook. Its private key goes straight to the controller on your Proxmox node.

Where should the runners work? For all of its repositories. You need to be an owner of the organization. For one of your repositories, which you choose when you install the App. Sign in to GitHub as that account.

GitHub shows you the App before it creates it. Then it sends you back here with a line for the installer.
